SAFETY CHECKLIST

What the contract enforces

  • Case IDs are normalized and duplicates are rejected.
  • Original evidence must come from two different HTTPS hostnames.
  • Validators independently refetch evidence and verify leader-proposed fields.
  • Every assessed record keeps a SHA-256 digest bound to source order.
  • A challenge requires a third hostname and must arrive before the deadline.
  • Eligible finalization is permissionless so a missing filer cannot stall a case.
